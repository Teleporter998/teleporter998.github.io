I'm a generalist designer with 2+ years of experience in game development. My strongest areas are building, graphic design, and especially game design. I also work across UI/UX, logo & icon design, VFX, music & SFX, simple video editing, game testing, and 3D modeling & animation.

I use Unity as my main game engine and will be starting making my first game in it soon. I'm not currently making full games in Roblox Studio — instead I build tools there, including the Fireporter plugin. Outside of development I produce music and do video editing.

I own and primarily develop for Teleported Memories, a community of developers who make games together.
